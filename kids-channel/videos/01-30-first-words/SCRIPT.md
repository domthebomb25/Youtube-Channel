# Video 1 — Script

**Title:** 30 First Words for Toddlers | Learn to Talk with Miss Poppy | Toddler Learning Video
**Length:** ~10 minutes · **Voice:** Miss Poppy ("Gracie" voice, slowed) · **Format:** 16:9

## How to read this script

Every line has a tag that says how it gets made (and what it costs):

| Tag | What's on screen | Made with |
|---|---|---|
| **[TALK]** | Miss Poppy close-up, mouth moving with her words | MiniMax lip-sync (the cheaper version we tested) |
| **[SHOW]** | The toy / animal / object, moving on its own — **no narration** (sound effects / music only) | Cheap animation (Grok Lite) |
| **[WAIT]** | Miss Poppy smiling and waiting so your child can say the word (no talking) | Cheap animation |
| **[REUSE]** | Hello song, goodbye song, "Good job!" cheers, confetti — made once, used in every video | Made once in video 1 |

The word also appears in big friendly letters on screen every time Miss Poppy says it (added in the edit — free).

**The teaching loop used for every word** (the Ms Rachel-style speech method):
show it → name it slowly → "Can you say ___?" → **wait 3 seconds** → say it again → cheer.

## Notes
- **Songs:** the hello and goodbye songs are original lyrics (no copyright issues). They need a simple music track — I'll quote that cost separately before making it.
- **Pauses:** every **[WAIT]** is 3 seconds of silence — this is when the child talks. Don't cut them.
- **Speech:** "Speech practice," never "therapy." Mouth-shape tips ("lips together," "round lips") are general modeling, as Ms Rachel does.
