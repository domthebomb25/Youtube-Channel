# Miss Poppy's Playroom: channel descriptions

## YouTube (Studio → Customization → Basic info → Description)

Welcome to Miss Poppy's Playroom! 🖍️🌈

Miss Poppy helps babies and toddlers learn to talk through play, songs and lots of happy repetition. Every video is slow, gentle and made for little learners, with plenty of pauses so your child has time to say the words too.

In our toddler learning videos, your little one will practice:
🗣️ First words: mama, dada, ball, more, bye-bye and more
🐮 Animal names and animal sounds
🎨 Colors, shapes and counting
🎵 Nursery rhymes and kids songs
👋 Simple baby signs, waving, clapping and pointing

Miss Poppy uses simple speech practice ideas parents can copy at home: say a word slowly, wait, and celebrate every try. These educational videos for toddlers work best when you watch together, so sing along, copy the actions and cheer your little one on!

👶 Made for babies, toddlers and preschoolers (ages 0–4)
📅 New baby learning videos every week
🔔 Subscribe so you never miss a new video

Miss Poppy is an animated character, made with the help of AI animation tools. Videos are for learning and fun and are not a substitute for professional speech therapy. If you have concerns about your child's speech, please talk to your pediatrician or a licensed speech-language pathologist.
