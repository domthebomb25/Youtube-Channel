# Miss Poppy's Playroom: channel descriptions

## YouTube (Studio → Customization → Basic info → Description)

Welcome to Miss Poppy's Playroom! 🖍️🌈

Miss Poppy helps babies and toddlers learn to talk through play, songs and happy repetition. Every video is slow and gentle, with pauses so your child has time to say the words too.

Your little one will practice:
🗣️ First words: mama, dada, ball, more, bye-bye
🐮 Animal names and sounds
🎨 Colors, shapes and counting
🎵 Nursery rhymes and kids songs
👋 Baby signs, waving and pointing

These educational videos for toddlers work best when you watch together, so sing along and cheer your little one on!

👶 For babies, toddlers and preschoolers (ages 0–4)
📅 New toddler learning videos every week
🔔 Subscribe so you never miss a video

Miss Poppy is an animated character made with AI animation tools. Videos are for learning and fun, not a substitute for speech therapy. If you're concerned about your child's speech, talk to your pediatrician or a speech-language pathologist.
