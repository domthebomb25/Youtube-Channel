# Hotel overbooking (shorts/day02/hotel-overbooking)

Locked script (84 words, ~28s). Voice: Benji preset (text2speech_v2 / elevenlabs). Numbers are spelled out for the voice
("one hundred", "one hundred ten") and shown as digits in the captions.

1. Your hotel sold your room twice — on purpose.
2. It is not a mistake. It is the plan.
3. A hotel with 100 rooms might sell 110.
4. But here's the trick. They are betting some guests never show up. A room that sits empty tonight makes no money.
5. Most nights the bet wins. But when everyone shows up, one guest gets walked — sent to another hotel, and the first hotel pays for everything.
6. And the front desk can smile through all of it because —
