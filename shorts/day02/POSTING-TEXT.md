## southwest-arm-wrestle

**YouTube title (73):** Southwest Settled a Lawsuit With an Arm-Wrestling Match #shorts #business

**Description:**
In 1992, Southwest Airlines and Stevens Aviation both used a very similar slogan ("Just Plane Smart" and "Plane Smart"). Instead of going to court, the two bosses settled it with an arm-wrestling match. Southwest's Herb Kelleher lost, but both companies kept using the slogan, and money went to charity. Reports differ on some details of the deal, and one report says the sides agreed on the outcome beforehand.
