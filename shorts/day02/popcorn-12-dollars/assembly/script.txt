Why does movie popcorn cost twelve dollars? It is not really about the popcorn. It is about your ticket. In the first weeks, the studio takes up to ninety cents of every ticket dollar. But here's the trick. The movie is just there to get you inside. Once you are in, the popcorn pays for everything. A bucket that costs less than a dollar to make sells for twelve.
