Here is the real truth. Movie theaters don't make their money on tickets. Studios take a big share of ticket sales, often half or more, depending on the movie. But theaters keep all of the snack money. At big chains, snacks bring in roughly a third of the revenue, and the profit margin on them is reported to top eighty percent. That's why the popcorn is so expensive: it's where the theater makes its money. The tickets keep the doors open, and the popcorn pays the bills.
