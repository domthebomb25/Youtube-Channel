You might disagree with this, but a lawsuit isn't always the answer. It's nineteen ninety-two, and you run Southwest Airlines. A small company called Stevens Aviation says your slogan, "Just Plane Smart," is too close to its own, "Plane Smart." So their CEO makes an offer: settle it with an arm-wrestling match, best two out of three. Your boss, Herb Kelleher, says yes. He lost the match, but both companies kept using the slogan, and money went to charity. No courtroom, just one very sore arm.
