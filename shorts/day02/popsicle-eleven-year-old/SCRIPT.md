# The Popsicle

Hook: You need to hear this…
Title on hook: AN 11-YEAR-OLD INVENTED THE POPSICLE

You need to hear this. The Popsicle was invented by an eleven-year-old. As the story goes, in nineteen oh five, Frank Epperson left a cup of soda powder and water with a stirring stick outside overnight. It froze solid, and he had a frozen treat on a stick. His kids later called it "Pop's sicle," and the name stuck. Think about that: one forgotten cup turned into a treat people are still buying more than a hundred years later. The idea came from a mistake, and the stick was the genius part.
