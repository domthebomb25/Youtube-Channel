# Amazon Relentless

Hook: Pause for a second…
Title on hook: RELENTLESS.COM → AMAZON?

Pause for a second. Type "relentless dot com" into your browser, and it takes you straight to Amazon. Why? In nineteen ninety-four, Jeff Bezos bought that name and almost used it for his store. Friends said it sounded sinister. Before that, the company was called Cadabra, until a lawyer reportedly heard "cadaver." So he went with Amazon, after the river. He kept the Relentless website, though, and honestly, it fits. Amazon dropped the name Relentless, but they're still relentless about one thing: your cart.
