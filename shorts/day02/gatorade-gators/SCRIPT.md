# Gatorade

Hook: Did you know…
Title on hook: GATORADE IS NAMED AFTER A FOOTBALL TEAM

Did you know Gatorade is named after a college football team? In nineteen sixty-five, the University of Florida Gators were struggling in the summer heat. A team of Florida researchers, led by Dr. Robert Cade, created a drink to help the players recover, and the team named it Gatorade. Then a company called Stokely-Van Camp started selling it everywhere. Eventually, Quaker Oats owned it, and PepsiCo bought Quaker in two thousand one. A team's heat problem turned into one of the biggest sports drinks in the world.
