## Facts and sources (checked Oct 14 2026)
- $50M offer in 2000, declined; "struggling not to laugh" is Marc Randolph's account (That Will Never Work): CNBC 2020-09-22, Fortune 2023-04-14, InsideHook, Inc. excerpt.
- Nearly $800M late fees = 16% of revenue in 2000: NBC News, Business Insider (secondary reporting, not found in Blockbuster's own filings).
- $134M late fees (3% of revenue) before bankruptcy: NBC News (year not given, so undated).
- 9,094 stores in 2004: Wikipedia; Sound & Vision "over 9,000". Late fees ended Jan 1 2005 (CBS/Newsweek). Chapter 11 September 2010 (Dish buy coverage). Netflix market cap about $300-345B mid-2026.
- Left out on purpose: 2004 $1.2B loss (mostly non-cash), $145M vs $200M late-fee cost (sources conflict), "$ lost per day" (no honest figure).
