Blockbuster vs Netflix: in 2000, Netflix's founders offered to sell the company to Blockbuster for $50 million. Blockbuster said no.

That year, Blockbuster collected nearly $800 million in late fees, reportedly 16% of its revenue. Netflix co-founder Marc Randolph says Blockbuster's CEO was struggling not to laugh at the offer. Blockbuster filed for bankruptcy in September 2010. Netflix is now worth hundreds of billions.
