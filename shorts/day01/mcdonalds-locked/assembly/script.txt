The McDonald's ice cream machine isn't broken. It's locked. Every night it runs a four-hour heat cycle up to one hundred fifty-one degrees. If that cycle fails, it locks until a tech comes. Federal agencies say that can cost six hundred twenty-five dollars a day in lost sales. Only Taylor techs can unlock it, for over three hundred dollars per fifteen minutes, the agencies say. So startup Kytch built a device that shows what's wrong on your phone. McDonald's told owners to remove it, citing safety risks. Kytch says that's false, and sued for nine hundred million dollars. McDonald's calls the claims meritless. So next time it says broken it might just be locked.
