You're not going to agree with this… Kleenex wasn't made for noses. After World War One, Kimberly-Clark was stuck with leftover wartime material. So in nineteen twenty-four, it turned it into a soft tissue and sold it as a cold-cream remover. Hollywood stars used it to wipe off their makeup. Then the letters started coming in. Customers said they were blowing their noses with it. So the company changed its pitch: Don't carry a cold in your pocket. The customers invented the product's real use. Kleenex is now worth billions, thanks to their customers.
