Nobody talks about this… Mountain Dew wasn't meant to be a soda. The nineteen forties, Knoxville, Tennessee. Two brothers can't find a lemon-lime mixer they like for their whiskey. So they make their own. They call it Mountain Dew, old slang for moonshine. The joke? Mixing with whiskey still tastes good to this day. Years later, it was sold as a soda for everyone. One of America's top-selling sodas, named after moonshine.
