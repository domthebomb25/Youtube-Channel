MrBeast lost eighty million dollars... and it wasn't an accident. He makes hundreds of millions on YouTube, but spends even MORE on private islands, massive stunt crews, and crazy giveaways. To any regular business, losing eighty million dollars sounds like immediate bankruptcy. But MrBeast isn't worried. Because he isn't trying to make money on YouTube videos. He uses his massive view counts as a giant, zero dollar advertisement for his real business. His chocolate brand, Feastables, brings in over two hundred million dollars in sales, turning those video losses into a multi-billion dollar empire. Turns out, losing millions on YouTube was just the entry fee. Which is why...
