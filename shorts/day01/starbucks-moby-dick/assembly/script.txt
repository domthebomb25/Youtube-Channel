Did you know Starbucks exists thanks to Moby Dick? Here's how. Seattle, nineteen seventy-one. Three founders are opening a coffee shop, and they need a name. One idea comes from Moby-Dick: Pequod, the name of the whaling ship. As the story goes, someone said, Nobody wants a cup of Pequod. So they picked the ship's first mate instead. His name? Starbuck. Add an S, and you get one of the biggest coffee brands on Earth. A multi-billion-dollar empire, thanks to Moby Dick.
