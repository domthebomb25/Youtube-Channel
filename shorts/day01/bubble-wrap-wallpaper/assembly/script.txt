Pause for a second… Bubble Wrap was invented as wallpaper? Nineteen fifty-seven. Two inventors seal two shower curtains together, trapping air bubbles inside. Their plan? Textured wallpaper for your home. Nobody wanted it. So they tried greenhouse insulation. That flopped too. Then they found its real job: protecting fragile things in boxes. And the real reason you remember it? You can't stop popping it. A failed wallpaper became the sound we all love.
