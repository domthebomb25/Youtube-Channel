The airport trapped you inside a mall — on purpose. Planes bring you in. Then the shops take over. About four of every ten airport dollars never come from planes. Pass security, you can't leave — and shops pay big rent for exactly that. Flight delayed? You wait longer — and every extra minute, the shops make more. Airports also make six billion dollars off cars just sitting there — more than the four point three billion dollars planes pay to land.
