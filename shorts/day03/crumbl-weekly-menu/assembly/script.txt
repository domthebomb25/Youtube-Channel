Ever notice this pattern? Crumbl's menu keeps changing. Two cousins started it in Logan, Utah, in twenty seventeen, and rotating flavors became its trademark. Miss one, and it might not come back for a while. Analysts call that manufactured scarcity. It gives you a reason to come back, and fans a new cookie to post about. Crumbl has since cut how many flavors rotate each week, but the drop is still the main event. Crumbl is crumbling the old rule: keep the menu the same.
