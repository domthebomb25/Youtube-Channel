# The tiny hole in airplane windows

Format: Sounds dumb until it's genius
Hook: Here is the real truth.
Title on hook: THAT TINY HOLE IN YOUR WINDOW

Here is the real truth. That tiny hole in your airplane window is not a mistake. Airplane windows have three layers, and the hole sits in the middle one. It lets cabin pressure reach the gap next to the outer layer, so the outer pane carries the load while the middle pane stands by as backup. Engineers also say the hole helps keep moisture from fogging the window. That tiny hole isn't a flaw. It's part of the plan.
