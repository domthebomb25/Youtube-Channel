# Coke's contour bottle

Format: Sounds dumb until it's genius
Hook: Nobody talks about this.
Title on hook: COKE'S BOTTLE LOOKS LIKE A POD

Nobody talks about this. Over a hundred years ago, Coca-Cola asked bottle makers for a design so distinctive you could recognize it by touch in the dark, or even if it was smashed on the ground. A glass company in Indiana won, and patented the shape in nineteen fifteen. As the story goes, the designers looked up coca and kola in a book, found a cocoa pod instead, and based the curves on it. A happy accident, and that shape is still one of the most famous in the world.

Sources: Wikipedia (Earl R. Dean, Root Glass Company), FoodNavigator (contour bottle 100 years), History by Mail (patent Nov 16 1915). Contest year (1914 vs 1915) and the cocoa-pod story differ by source, so the script says 'over a hundred years ago' and 'as the story goes'.
