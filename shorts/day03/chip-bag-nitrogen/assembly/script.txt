Have you ever noticed your chip bag is mostly air? It's not really air. It's mostly nitrogen. Oxygen makes the oils in chips go stale, so companies flush most of it out and seal the bag with nitrogen instead. The gas also works like a cushion, so the chips don't get crushed on the way to the store. Critics say the extra space also makes the bag look fuller than it is. So next time you open a puffy bag, remember: it's chip insurance.
