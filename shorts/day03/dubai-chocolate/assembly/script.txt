Stop scrolling for a second. One chocolate bar helped make pistachios harder to find. It started with a British-Egyptian entrepreneur in Dubai who turned her knafeh craving into a bar, filled with pistachio cream and crunchy pastry strands. Sales were slow, until an influencer posted a crunchy video of it. The founders say that brought at least thirty thousand orders. Copycats followed, and in twenty twenty-five pistachio prices reportedly jumped, helped along by a weak harvest. One crunch really did shake up the market.
