Pause for a second. Why was Hollister so dark inside? The stores were known for dim lights, loud music and heavy cologne, more like a club than a shop. A worker once told a reporter the mood was meant to make you stay and hang out. But some shoppers complained they couldn't see prices or sizes. Later, as sales fell, the company started brightening the stores and toning down the music and cologne. Shoppers wanted the vibe and the price tag. Hollister had to give them both.
