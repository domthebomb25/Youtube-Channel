## 1. chip-bag-nitrogen

**YouTube title:** The Shocking Secret of Chip Bags (It's Not Just Air) #shorts #business

**Description:**
That puffy chip bag is mostly nitrogen, not just air. Nitrogen replaces most of the oxygen, which helps keep the oils in chips from going stale, and the gas also cushions the chips so they don't get crushed on the way to the store. Critics say the extra space also makes the bag look fuller than it is. Fill levels vary by brand.

## 2. coke-contour-bottle

**YouTube title:** The Genius of Coke's Bottle (As the Story Goes) #shorts #business

**Description:**
Over a hundred years ago, Coca-Cola asked bottle makers for a design so distinctive it could be recognized by touch in the dark, or even if it was broken. The Root Glass Company of Terre Haute, Indiana, won, and the shape was patented in 1915. As the story goes, the designers looked up coca and kola in a book, found a cocoa pod instead, and based the curves on it. Sources differ on the details, including the contest year and what book they used, so the pod story is the popular explanation, not a confirmed fact.

## 3. crumbl-weekly-menu

**YouTube title:** Crumbl's Secret Marketing Tactic: Manufactured Scarcity #shorts #business

**Description:**
Crumbl was started in Logan, Utah, in 2017 by two cousins, and its rotating cookie flavors became its trademark. Miss a flavor and it might not come back for a while. Analysts call that manufactured scarcity: it gives customers a reason to come back and fans a new cookie to post about. Crumbl has not officially explained its reasoning. In January 2026 Crumbl cut how many flavors rotate each week, to six classics plus four rotating flavors.

## 4. hollister-dim-lights

**YouTube title:** Why Does Hollister Dim the Lights? #shorts #business

**Description:**
Hollister stores were known for dim lights, loud music and heavy cologne, more like a club than a shop. One worker told a reporter the mood was meant to make shoppers stay and hang out, and some shoppers complained they could not see prices or sizes. Later, as sales fell, parent company Abercrombie & Fitch started brightening stores and toning down the music and cologne. The company has not given a single official reason for the dim lighting, and the exact timing of the changes is reported differently.

## 5. dubai-chocolate

**YouTube title:** Who Created Dubai Chocolate? One Bar Sparked a Pistachio Rush #shorts #business

**Description:**
Dubai chocolate began with Fix Dessert Chocolatier in Dubai, where British-Egyptian entrepreneur Sarah Hamouda turned a knafeh craving into a chocolate bar filled with pistachio cream and crunchy pastry strands. ## 6. airplane-window-hole

**YouTube title:** Airplane's Secret Window Hole, Explained #shorts #business

**Description:**
The tiny hole in an airplane window is called a bleed or breather hole, and it is on purpose. Airplane windows have three layers, and the hole sits in the middle one. It lets cabin pressure reach the gap next to the outer layer, so the outer pane carries the load while the middle pane stands by as a backup. Engineers also say the hole helps keep moisture from fogging the window.
