# Hook templates (from the user — proven to grab attention)

Use these as the opening line / first slide / first on-screen text for slideshows, clips, intros, parts and titles.
Fill the blank with the video's most surprising fact. Rotate them — don't repeat the same hook two days in a row.

- Stop scrolling for a second…
- Here is the hard truth…
- Have you ever noticed that ___?
- This might surprise you…
- You need to hear this…
- I just realized something…
- You might disagree…
- I wish somebody told me this sooner…
- Let me save you some time…
- Nobody talks about this…
- Nobody mentions this…
- I wish I knew this earlier…
- Pause for a second…
- Ever notice this pattern?
- Here is the real truth…
- You're not going to agree with this…

Examples (Costco): "Nobody talks about this: Costco loses money on its $1.50 hot dog… on purpose."
"Have you ever noticed the rotisserie chicken is always at the back of the store?"
