# THE BUSINESS STICK HQ — SHORT / REEL SCRIPT TEMPLATE (the playbook)

Use for every daily short. One master video → YouTube Shorts + Facebook Reel + Instagram + TikTok. Targets it is built to hit: beat the 9.4s average exit (goal 15.4s+ avg watch time), drive comments + follows, protect Facebook Content Monetization eligibility. **Length: 35–45 seconds · ~105–120 spoken words · vertical 1080×1920**

## THE SCRIPT SKELETON (copy this table for every video)

| Time | Beat | What goes here | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | **THE HOOK** | DEFAULT: one searchable QUESTION, spoken AND on screen: "Why did ___ ?" / "How did ___ lose $___?" VARIANT (~3 in 10): a shock STATEMENT when the bare fact beats any question ("Blockbuster could have bought Netflix for $50M"). | Same words spoken + shown (SEO). Either form must contain the money mystery. Test: say both versions aloud — whichever stops a stranger mid-scroll wins; if it's close, take the question. No intro, no greeting, no logo. |
| 0:03–0:10 | **THE SETUP** | The surprising origin / situation in 1–2 lines. Weirdest true detail goes here. | Viewer must think "wait, what?" by second 8. |
| 0:10–0:17 | **THE STAKES ★** | The money line: dollar figure, scale, or "still selling after ___ years" — the business mystery stated out loud. | **Non-negotiable.** The average Facebook viewer leaves at ~9.4s — the stakes must land BEFORE that, never save them for the payoff. |
| 0:17–0:27 | **THE TURN** | "But here's the trick / the catch / where it went wrong…" — the mechanism starts. New visual or on-screen text every ~5 seconds (pattern interrupt). | One idea per line. Pauses between lines ≈ 0.2s — never let the energy dip. |
| 0:27–0:37 | **THE PAYOFF** | The full money mechanic: how they actually make / made / lost the money. End on the sharpest fact. | Every fact verified before animating. If a dollar figure can't be verified, leave it out — never invent numbers. |
| 0:37–0:43 | **THE PERFECT-LOOP ENDING** | The final spoken line is a bridge back into the hook, over the visual that matches the opening frame. It can be a causal or unfinished line the hook completes, or a question only when the hook directly answers it without a hard stop. | **Perfect loop comes first.** Do not force a standalone viewer question into the narration if it creates a full stop before the restart. Put the debate question in the caption/pinned comment, or as small on-screen text, unless it preserves the seam. |

## LOOP RULES (every video, no exceptions)
1. **First frame = last frame.** Same scene, same composition (e.g., product alone on the shelf). The restart must be invisible.
2. **Circular writing:** the last spoken line must flow into the hook — the hook should feel like the answer or the continuation on rewatch. **Seam test:** read the last line and the first line back-to-back; together they should sound like one continuous thought, with no closing full stop in the narration.
3. **No endings that announce themselves:** no fade-out, no end card, no logo outro, no "SUBSCRIBE FOR MORE" banner. Endings kill the second watch.
4. **Tight tail:** trim trailing silence to under ~0.3s. Loop the music bed under the cut so the audio doesn't hiccup.
5. **CTA lives on screen only, small, in the final seconds:** `FOLLOW FOR TOMORROW'S STORY` + a second line naming tomorrow's story: `Tomorrow: [next story's hook question]`.

## ON-SCREEN & VISUAL RULES
- **Character:** Business Stick — big round white head, thick black outline, white collared shirt, gold tie — on royal blue. Branding text yellow or white only.
- **Captions ON, burned in,** word-for-word with the narration (silent viewers + SEO).
- **On-screen text = the keywords:** every beat gets a 2–4 word text pop (NEVER FINISH / PATENTED 1872 / SAME JELLY ×4). The words on screen should be the words people would search.
- **Say it AND show it in the first 3 seconds** — hook words are spoken, captioned, and popped as text simultaneously.
- No public numbering on shorts (no "Story #23"). Part 1 / Part 2 ONLY for genuine multi-part arcs. Number files internally instead.

## TITLE & CAPTIONS (one video, three wrappers)
**YouTube Shorts title:** the hook question, exactly as spoken. Description + tags per vidIQ research.

**Facebook / Instagram caption (short format):**

> [Hook question — must land in the first 10–12 words] [One-line payoff tease.]
>
> [Question to the audience]? 👇
>
> Follow @thebusinessstickhq for a new business story every day 👔
>
> #businessstories #[topic] (FB: 2–3 hashtags total · IG: up to 6, add #businessstory #entrepreneur + topic tags)

**Instagram only:** add a location tag when the story has a famous one (e.g., Costco → Costco Iwilei, Honolulu).

**Pinned comment (write BEFORE posting, post the second it goes live):** a debate question with YOUR take stated first — "Genius or madness? I say genius — [reason]. Change my mind 👇"

## POSTING CHECKLIST (every post, in order)
- [ ] Clean master uploaded natively to Facebook — never a TikTok/YouTube watermarked copy, never a link post
- [ ] **AI label: ON** for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok · **OFF** for YouTube
- [ ] Pinned comment posted immediately; reply to every comment in the first hour
- [ ] Shared as the Page into 1–3 business/entrepreneur Groups
- [ ] Filed into its Facebook Playlist (💰 How They Make Money / 😳 Big Business Mistakes & Wild Deals / 🤯 Brand Stories & Weird Origins)
- [ ] One story per day — never repost a duplicate; one best version of every story
- [ ] File saved as `BS-###-slug.mp4` (internal numbering)

## MONETIZATION GUARDRAILS (baked into every script)
- 100% original: own character, own script, own researched story — this is the defense against "inauthentic/reused content" flags.
- Minimum 10 seconds (Facebook Reels eligibility) — the 35–45s pocket clears it easily.
- Facts verified before production (the Alphabet rule: one wrong number can cost a whole video — check every figure).
- Platform-neutral spoken CTA — never say "subscribe" in the video itself; that word only belongs in YouTube's wrapper.

## THE FORMULA IN ONE LINE
**Question hook (0–3s) → weird setup (3–10s) → money stakes before 10s is out → the turn → the payoff → perfect-loop bridge back into the hook, looping forever.**
