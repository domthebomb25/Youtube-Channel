This might surprise you. The biggest tire maker in the world isn't Michelin. It isn't Goodyear. It's LEGO. Think about it. Cars, trucks, tractors, race cars, even spaceships, almost every set with wheels needs tiny rubber tires. And LEGO sells a lot of sets. Every year, LEGO makes more than three hundred million tires. In twenty ten, it hit three hundred eighty one million. That's more tires than any real tire company makes in a year. Guinness World Records made it official: LEGO is the world's biggest tire maker, by number. Of course, a real car tire is a little bigger. So the tire giants still win on rubber, but LEGO wins on count. So next time you step on a LEGO tire, you're stepping on a world record.
