**Plain narration:**
> This might surprise you. The biggest tire maker in the world isn't Michelin. It isn't Goodyear. It's LEGO. Think about it. Cars, trucks, tractors, race cars, even spaceships… almost every set with wheels needs tiny rubber tires. And LEGO sells a lot of sets. Every year, LEGO makes more than three hundred million tires. In 2010, it hit three hundred eighty-one million. That's more tires than any real tire company makes in a year. Guinness World Records made it official: LEGO is the world's biggest tire maker… by number. Of course, a real car tire is a little bigger. So the tire giants still win on rubber… but LEGO wins on count. So next time you step on a LEGO tire… you're stepping on a world record.

## Fact check (sources)
- Guinness World Records recognizes the LEGO Group as the largest tire manufacturer by number of tires. ✅
- Output peaked at **381 million** tires in **2010** (Guinness). Recent years are reported around **300+ million** (European Rubber Journal ~318M; other sources ~306M), so the script says "more than three hundred million." ✅
- Real tire makers produce far fewer per year (e.g., Michelin ~200 million). Script only claims "more than any real tire company," not exact competitor numbers. ✅
- Caveat (kept honest in the script): they're tiny toy tires — "wins on count," not on rubber. ✅

Sources: Safelite "The World's Largest Tire Manufacturer May Surprise You" · European Rubber Journal "And the world's biggest tyre maker is… Lego" · Bush Tyres "And the Highest Producing Tyre Manufacturer is…"
