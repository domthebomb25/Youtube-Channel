Why is a Costco hot dog still $1.50 after 40 years? A top Costco executive once pushed to raise the price — and co-founder Jim Sinegal's answer became business legend: "If you raise the price of the hot dog, I will kill you."

Here's the real reason the price never moves (it was never about the hot dog).
