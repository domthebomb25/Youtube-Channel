Have you ever noticed the McDonald's ice cream machine is always broken? It's not your imagination. And the reason is wild. Every night, the machine runs a heat cleaning cycle to kill bacteria. It can take up to four hours. If anything goes wrong, it locks itself, and shows error codes workers can't make sense of. So the restaurant has to call a technician approved by the company that makes the machine. Then a tiny startup built a gadget that decoded the errors. Owners loved it. McDonald's told franchise owners to stop using it. The startup sued for nine hundred million dollars. Even the FTC started asking questions. In twenty twenty four, the government finally made it legal for outside repair shops to unlock these machines. So next time it's broken it might just be cleaning or still waiting on the repair guy.
