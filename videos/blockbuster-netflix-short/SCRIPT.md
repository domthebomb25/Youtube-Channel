# Blockbuster Short — "You're the company" format

**Format:** "You're the company" roleplay. Put the viewer in charge, give them a problem, let the story build, and reveal the company name at the end.
**Length:** ~40–45 seconds (~115 words) · **Vertical 9:16** · Voice: Benji (same as the Costco video)
**Hook template used:** "Pause for a second…" (from `channel/HOOK-TEMPLATES.md`)
**Rule:** don't say "Blockbuster" or "Netflix" until the last 8 seconds. Viewers stay to find out who it is.

**Plain narration (for the voiceover):**

> Pause for a second. You run the biggest video rental company on Earth. Thousands of stores. Packed every Friday night. Late fees alone make you hundreds of millions a year.
> Then two guys from a tiny startup fly in to meet you. Their company mails DVDs. It's losing money. And they want you to buy it… for fifty million dollars.
> Would you?
> You try not to laugh. You say no. They fly home.
> Then they get faster. Then they start streaming. And ten years later… you're bankrupt.
> That tiny startup was Netflix. It's now worth hundreds of billions.
> And you? You were Blockbuster.

## Why it's built this way

- **Hook (0:00–0:04):** puts the viewer in the CEO's seat right away, so they have something at stake. No statistic-only opening.
- **"Would you?" (0:21):** makes the viewer decide. Most people think "no," so the ending hits them personally.
- **Payoff (0:34–0:42):** the name reveal is the reward for watching to the end.
- **End question:** gets comments ("I would've bought it!"), which helps the Short get shown to more people.

## Fact check (verified before posting)

- 2000: Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph offered Netflix to Blockbuster for **$50 million**. Randolph has said CEO John Antioco was **"struggling not to laugh."** ✅
- Netflix was losing money at the time (mail-order DVDs). ✅
- Late fees were a huge share of Blockbuster's revenue (reported at hundreds of millions per year). ✅
- Blockbuster filed for bankruptcy in **2010**, ten years later. ✅
- Netflix is worth **hundreds of billions** today. ✅
