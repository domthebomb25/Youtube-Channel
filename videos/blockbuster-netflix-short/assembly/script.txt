Pause for a second. You run the biggest video rental company on Earth. Thousands of stores. Packed every Friday night. Late fees alone make you hundreds of millions a year. Then two guys from a tiny startup fly in to meet you. Their company mails DVDs. It's losing money. And they want you to buy it for fifty million dollars. Would you? You try not to laugh. You say no. They fly home. Then they get faster. Then they start streaming. And ten years later you're bankrupt. That tiny startup was Netflix. It's now worth hundreds of billions. And you? You were Blockbuster.
